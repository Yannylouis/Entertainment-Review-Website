The 100

Genre: Science Fiction

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

My Review

The 100 is an exciting TV series, that follows a group of teens that return to Earth after humanity has been living in space.
They face dangerous situations all throughout the show, while making harsh and difficult choices to protect the human race.

Final Opinion

Overall, the 100 is one of mine and many other's favorite TV series because of the supense and unexpected plot twist the show brings to its audience.
This series also portrays different character developments as they deal with difficult situations. Causing some of the most hated characters to become people's favorites.
Unfortunately, the 100's season 7 completly abandoned the show's storyline which made it the worst season for many fans.

Final Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐½
